We've been running Sacred Tree Service since early 2023, working trees across Orlando and the surrounding 50 miles of Central Florida. We started this blog for one reason: most of the tree-care content online for Florida homeowners is bad.

It's either thin SEO filler or a pitch dressed up as advice. Neither helps you make a real decision about your trees.

## What you'll find here

We're going to write the stuff we actually tell people on estimates:

- **Species ID and care** — live oaks, sabal palms, magnolias, citrus, pines, the good and the bad of each
- **Storm prep** — what to do before hurricane season, and how to read damage after
- **When to remove vs. save** — the honest framework an arborist uses
- **What things actually cost** — and why two estimates can differ by 5x

## Who's writing

This blog is written by our team, including our **ISA Certified Arborists**. When a post is written by a credentialed arborist, you'll see the badge on the byline.

That's the deal. No fluff, no clickbait, just the kind of notes we wish more tree services published.

If you have a question you want covered, [reach out](/contact/) — we'll write about it.
